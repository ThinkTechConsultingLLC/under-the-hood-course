# How to use this site

Welcome! 👋 You're looking at the home of **Course 01 — How Computers & AI Really Work**. This page is a 2-minute map so you always know where you are and where to go next.

## Finding your way — the menu
Everything is in the **menu on the left** (on a phone, tap the **☰** icon top-left). It's the whole course, in order:

- **Home** — what the course is and the full module list
- **How to use this site** — this page
- **Start here** — what you need (almost nothing!) and how each session works
- **Syllabus** — the detailed plan
- **M1 → M12** — the twelve modules, in order
- **Resources** — glossary, cheat-card, and setup guides

## What's inside each module
Open any module (M1–M12) and you'll find three pages:

| Page | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| **Overview** | The one-screen plan for the session — your "win" and the run of show. Read this first. |
| **Notes** | The in-depth reading that explains the ideas. Read before or after the lab. |
| **Lab** | The hands-on, step-by-step part — **this is where the real learning happens.** Each step tells you exactly what you should see. |

## Doing the hands-on labs
The labs run in different places depending on the module — each lab tells you which, but in short:

- **M1–M2** → on **your own computer** (you're exploring *your* machine).
- **M3–M10** → in a **Codespace** — a free Linux computer that runs **in your browser**, nothing to install. Just sign in with a free GitHub account and click **[▶ Open in Codespaces](https://codespaces.new/ThinkTechConsultingLLC/under-the-hood-course)** (details in **Resources → "Lab · Codespaces"**).
- **M11–M12** → free **browser AI tools** (no setup).

## Resources (when you need them)
- **Glossary** — every technical word, in plain language. Stuck on a term? It's here.
- **Cheat-card** — the handful of commands you'll use day-to-day (you look them up, you don't memorize them).
- **Install guides** — *only* when a module asks for one.

## If you get stuck
1. Re-read the **✅ "You should now see…"** line under the step you're on.
2. Ask your **breakout partner** — chances are they hit the same thing.
3. Post in the **class chat** — the instructor or a classmate will get you moving.

Nothing here can break your computer, and **getting things wrong is part of learning** — nobody's behind.

## Suggested path
First time? Read **Start here**, then go module by module, **M1 → M12**. Each one ends with a win. See you in Module 1. 🚀
